# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful,
harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of background, identity, experience,
or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give others room to learn.
- Respect privacy, especially around credentials, browser sessions, and page
  content.
- Put the safety of users and their data ahead of winning an argument.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, comments, or other material that violates it and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/s-block/browser-use-mcp/security/advisories/new).
If that form is unavailable, open a
[private-contact request](https://github.com/s-block/browser-use-mcp/issues/new?template=private_contact.yml).
Do not disclose sensitive conduct or personal details in the issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, consider the
context and impact, and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.

## Attribution
